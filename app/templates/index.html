Gel Band Analyzer

Measure every band on your gel in seconds

Drop in a gel or western blot. Lanes and bands are found and measured automatically, so you only check the result instead of drawing boxes by hand.

  1. Uploadlanes and bands are detected for you
  2. Checknudge any box that looks off and name your samples
  3. Exporta table, annotated images or a PDF report

Your images are stored on this server so you can come back to them. Anyone with a project's link can view and edit it, and projects are deleted after 30 days without changes.
